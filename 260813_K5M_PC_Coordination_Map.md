## Documents & Systems — how the coordination actually happens

- **COC** — "Center of Control," the master scope tracker per discipline (FFE / Fitout)
- **Material Package** — client-facing and supplier-facing versions, kept in sync from the COC
- **SHD Tracker** — shop-drawing review rounds: client feedback → internal review → factory
- **Task Tracker + Calendar** — every open item, owner, and deadline, one merged workbook
- **Fitout Scope** — DD-100 ↔ Ariel's resized set, cross-referenced item by item

## The scope split

- **FF&E scope** — loose furniture, fabrics, lighting, art — its own COC, Material Package, and SHD rounds.
- **Fitout scope** — built-in joinery, stone, wall panelling — its own COC, Material Package, and SHD rounds.

Almost everything above exists twice — once per scope. Always check which track you're in before you look for a document.
