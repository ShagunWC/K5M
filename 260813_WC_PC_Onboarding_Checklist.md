# First Month Onboarding Checklist — Project Coordinator

Source: `WC_PC_Onboarding Checklist Form.pdf` (WDCO standard onboarding form). Transcribed here for reference/tracking; the original signed PDF (with name/date/signature fields filled in) is the actual record of completion.

Each week ends with a Knowledge Transfer Acknowledgement: the Buddy/Mentor confirms knowledge was transferred, the New Project Coordinator confirms it was received, understood, and acknowledged — signed by both, dated.

## Week 1 — Folder Management & COC Familiarization

- [ ] Get access to shared drives/SharePoint
- [ ] Learn the folder naming convention and where each project's files live
- [ ] Understand what COC is
- [ ] Walk through a live COC file with a mentor and review at least 1 full project
- [ ] Learn status categories (Approved / Submitted / Pending / Rejected) and what triggers each
- [ ] Shadow someone updating a COC entry
- [ ] Know where to find: PO Tracker, Shop Drawing (SHD) status, Fabric & Material analysis sheets
- [ ] **End of week:** can locate any project's COC and explain its current status unaided

## Week 2 — Supplier Communication

- [ ] Get introduced to key active suppliers (by project/category)
- [ ] Learn the standard email/communication templates for supplier follow-ups
- [ ] Understand escalation path if a supplier misses a deadline
- [ ] Sit in on (or shadow) at least 2 live supplier calls/email threads
- [ ] Practice drafting a status-request or follow-up email (reviewed before sending)
- [ ] Learn how supplier updates should be logged back into COC/PO Tracker
- [ ] **End of week:** can independently draft and send a routine supplier follow-up

## Week 3 — Schedule Alignment

- [ ] Review the master project schedule(s) and how they link to COC/PO milestones
- [ ] Learn how delays in materials/fabrics/shop drawings cascade into schedule risk
- [ ] Sit with PM to review one project's full timeline end-to-end
- [ ] Practice flagging a schedule risk based on COC data
- [ ] Understand internal reporting rhythm (daily/weekly check-ins, dashboards, who needs to be updated)
- [ ] **End of week:** can read a project schedule and identify what's at risk without prompting

## Week 4 — Integration & Client-Readiness (if required)

- [ ] Demonstrate independent use of folder/COC system (no assistance needed)
- [ ] Demonstrate independent supplier follow-up (drafted + sent, reviewed after the fact only)
- [ ] Demonstrate ability to explain schedule status on a live project
- [ ] Shadow one client-facing call or email thread
- [ ] Draft one client-facing update (reviewed before sending)
- [ ] Manager/mentor sign-off: ready to handle routine client communication independently
- [ ] Debrief: gaps identified, follow-up training scheduled if needed
